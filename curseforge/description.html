GearGuide: Forever

Your next gear upgrade, and exactly where to get it. GearGuide is a best-in-slot (BiS) and gear upgrade planner for World of Warcraft: Forever. It recommends the best gear for your class, spec and level, shows which creature, quest, vendor or dungeon boss gives each item and how often it drops, and lets you plan whole sets before you go and get them. No other addons needed. Free and open source.

Features

Recommended upgrades: the best items for your spec in every slot, with the score gain over what you wear. Stat weights for all 28 specs (Haste and Expertise included, updated for Forever's class hotfixes), only the right armor type and weapon setup, spec detected from your talents or picked by you, or your own custom stat weights.

Where to get it: drops with drop chances, quest rewards, vendors and prices (Honor and tokens too), chests and crafted items. Click a source to open the world map with the area marked. BoE world drops show which creatures of which levels can drop them.

Upgrade numbers everywhere: on item icons in your bags, the bank, merchants, loot and quest rewards, and in item tooltips: how much better (green) or worse (red) the item is than what you wear. Works with Bagnon and other bag addons, and with any quest window.

Creature tooltips: mouse over a creature to see the gear it drops, with drop chances and the upgrade each piece would be, including the BoE world drops it can carry. Choose which drops show: upgrades only, gear your class can use, or all gear, by quality and level bracket.

Build Simulator: try on any gear and see your character sheet with it: your character wears the set, and health, attack power, weapon damage, crit, hit, dodge and more update as you swap items.

  • Recommended build: fills every slot with the best gear for your level. From level 50 the set is tuned as a whole for the hit, expertise and defense caps.
  • Where to get this set: the items you still need, grouped by dungeon, raid or zone.
  • Saved and shared builds: keep up to 20 named sets per character, and share a set with a short text a friend can import.
  • Inspect in GearGuide: open another player's gear in the simulator from the Inspect window.

Gear Browser: search every item by class, spec, slot, level range and quality, or pick a dungeon, raid or zone to see everything you can get there. All of Forever's new dungeons and zones are included.

Mini tracker: a small, movable list of your top upgrades. In a zone or dungeon it shows what you can get there and where. Hover mode hides everything but the list.

Loot roll help: on group loot rolls, see what the item is worth to you, and who in your party it's an upgrade for (they don't need GearGuide).

And more: favorites, Ignore for items you never want recommended, a pop-up when you get a recommended item, tracking of what you already have, faction-aware sources, color themes and window borders, and new sources learned as you play (optionally shared with other GearGuide users on your realm).

Getting started

Install with the CurseForge app to get every update automatically.

/gg or left-click the minimap icon: main window. Shift-click or /gg mini: mini tracker. /gg settings: settings.

Privacy and Blizzard's add-on policy

GearGuide never plays for you: it doesn't cast, equip, loot, buy, sell or chat. It collects no personal data; the optional source sharing sends only item IDs, source names and zones to other GearGuide users (realm, guild and group, through invisible addon messages and a hidden chat channel), never your inventory, gear, level, gold or name. Turn it off with the Sharing button or /gg sharing off. No ads, fully readable code.
