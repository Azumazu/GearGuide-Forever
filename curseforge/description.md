# GearGuide

**Know your next upgrade and exactly where to get it.** GearGuide recommends the best gear for your
class, spec and level in **World of Warcraft: Forever**, shows where every item comes from, and lets
you plan whole sets before you go and get them. No other addons needed. Free and open source.

## Features

**Recommended upgrades**: the best items for your spec in every slot, with the score gain over what
you wear. Stat weights for all 28 specs (Haste and Expertise included), only the right armor type and
weapon setup, spec detected from your talents or picked by you, or your own custom stat weights.

**Where to get it**: drops with drop chances, quest rewards, vendors and prices (Honor and tokens
too), chests and crafted items. Click a source to open the world map at that spot.

**Build Simulator**: try on any gear and see your character sheet with it: your character wears the
set, and health, attack power, weapon damage, crit, hit, dodge and more update as you swap items.
- **Recommended build**: fills every slot with the best gear for your level. From level 50 the set is
  tuned as a whole for the hit, expertise and defense caps.
- **Where to get this set**: the items you still need, grouped by dungeon, raid or zone.
- **Saved builds**: keep up to 20 named sets per character.

**Gear Browser**: search every item by class, spec, slot, level range and quality, or pick a dungeon
or raid to see its whole loot table.

**Mini tracker**: a small, movable list of your top upgrades. In a zone or dungeon it shows what drops
there and who drops it. Hover mode hides everything but the list.

**And more**: favorites, Ignore for items you never want recommended, a pop-up when you get a
recommended item, gear drops on creature tooltips, tracking of what you already have, faction-aware
sources, and new sources learned as you play (optionally shared with your guild and group).

## Getting started

`/gg` or left-click the minimap icon: main window. Shift-click or `/gg mini`: mini tracker.
`/gg settings`: settings.

## Privacy and Blizzard's add-on policy

GearGuide never plays for you: it doesn't cast, equip, loot, buy, sell or chat. It collects no
personal data; the optional source sharing sends only item and source names to your guild and group.
No ads, fully readable code.
