CurseForge image titles and descriptions (Project -> Images -> Upload, one per file)

1-recommended.png
  Title: Recommended upgrades
  Description: Best upgrades for your spec and level, grouped by slot, with the score gain over what
  you have equipped and where each item comes from, including drop chances.

2-gear-browser.png
  Title: Gear Browser
  Description: Search every item in the game by class, spec, slot, level range and quality, sorted by
  how good it is for you.

3-mini-tracker.png
  Title: Mini tracker
  Description: A small, movable list of your top upgrades. In a zone or dungeon it lists what drops
  there, who drops it and how often.

4-mini-tracker-hover-mode.png
  Title: Mini tracker - hover mode
  Description: Hover mode hides the header and background so only the list shows; they come back when
  you mouse over it.

5-build-simulator.png
  Title: Build Simulator
  Description: Try on any gear and see your stats with it. The Recommended build fills every slot with
  the best gear for your level, and "Where to get this set" lists what you still need, by dungeon,
  raid or zone. Save your sets as named builds.
